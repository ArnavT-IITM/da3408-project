Using AirCast

  1. The date at the top is "today" inside AirCast. The data is real, from 2020, so AirCast replays it one day at a time.
  2. Forecast shows tomorrow's air quality for each city, made from today's measurements.
  3. Next day moves time forward. Yesterday's forecasts are then checked against what really happened, and the Accuracy tab shows how close they were.
  4. Jump to goes straight to any date. Start over goes back to the beginning.
  5. Operations is for the person running the system: retrain the model, switch versions and check services.
